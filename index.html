The experience

What we’ll do in Israel

  • Meet high-profile speakers

    Meetings with companies, founders, investors, and other leaders.

  • Fun activities

    Beaches, Tel Aviv’s famous nightlife, and an unforgettable desert party.

  • Discover the culinary scene

    Carmel Market, Mahane Yehuda, and the best restaurants in town.

  • Explore history, culture, spirit & current events

    The Old City, Masada, and the Nova Festival memorial.

Itinerary

Where we’ll go

Three regions over eight days. Durations are approximate.

Tel Aviv-Yafo

3–4 days

Dive into Israel’s thriving innovation ecosystem, then enjoy the city’s beaches, food, nightlife, and energy.

  • Top VCs, startups, and tech leaders
  • The beach and Carmel Market
  • Tel Aviv’s well-known party scene
  • The best restaurants in the city

Jerusalem

2 days

Step into thousands of years of history: explore the Old City, visit iconic landmarks and holy sites, and experience the city’s unique culture.

  • The Old City and Mahane Yehuda market
  • The Western Wall and other religious sites
  • Food at the best restaurants

Dead Sea & the South

2 days

Experience the lowest point on Earth: float in the mineral-rich waters, cover yourself in Dead Sea mud, and take in the desert landscape.

  • Masada and the desert
  • Wellness at the Dead Sea
  • A desert party to close it out
  • The Nova Festival memorial

Safety

Your safety comes first

We understand that the current situation in Israel can be challenging, and we take your safety very seriously.

  • Fully refundable deposits

    In accordance with iTrek policies, deposits will be fully refundable.

  • We’ll cancel if it isn’t safe

    If the security situation does not improve and we don’t believe the trek can be conducted safely, we will cancel the trip.

  • Support on the ground

    24/7 security and a dedicated support team throughout the trek.
